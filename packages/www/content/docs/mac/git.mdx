## Branches

The branch picker is at the top of the sidebar. Click it to see your recent,
local and remote branches, and type to filter them. Arrow keys move through
the list and Return checks out the one you pick.

<Screenshot name="branch-picker" width={1470} height={1326} alt="The branch picker, with recent and local branches" />

Every branch has more actions behind the ellipsis at the end of its row, or a
right-click. Choose **New branch** at the top of the picker to create one.

The **Branches** surface in the bottom pane shows the same branches with more
room. Local branches are grouped by folder, and each one shows how far it is
ahead of or behind its upstream. Double-click a branch to check it out.

## Committing

While you review your own uncommitted changes, the commit box sits under the
changed files in the sidebar.

1. Tick the files you want in the commit.
2. Write the message, or click ✦ to have Reviewer write it from what changed.
3. Click **Commit**, or **Commit & push**.

Fetch, pull and push are in the command palette (⌘K), under Git.

## History

The **History** surface in the bottom pane is the repository's log, with its
branch graph.

<Screenshot name="history" width={1854} height={1192} alt="The history, with its branch graph and filters" />

The bar above the list filters it by:

- The branch the log follows.
- A file.
- Text or a commit hash. Toggle regular expressions and case sensitivity
  beside it.
- An author.
- A start date.

Click a commit to open its diff on the page. The details beside the list show
its message, author, date and the files it touched.

To follow a single file, right-click it in the tree and choose
**Show History**. History opens filtered to that file.
