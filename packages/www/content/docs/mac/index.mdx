## Opening a repository

The first time you open Reviewer, it shows the **opener**: every git
repository on your Mac, found by searching your home folder. Recent ones are
at the top, and folders holding several repositories get their own entry in
the sidebar.

Double-click a repository, or select it and press Return, to open it. To get
back to the opener later, press ⌘O or click the project name at the top of
the sidebar.

If a repository is missing, click the folder button in the opener's toolbar
to add it, or rescan to search your home folder again.

## The window

The window has four parts:

- **The sidebar** on the left. It holds the branch picker, the comparison
  picker, and the files: the whole project while you browse, or only the
  changed files while you review.
- **The page** in the middle. This is the diff, a file, or an agent session.
- **The tabs** in the toolbar. **Code** and **Sessions** are always there,
  followed by one tab for each agent session you have open.
- **The bottom pane**, under the page. It holds Branches, History, Terminal
  and Run. Press ⌘B to show or hide it.

Press ⌃⌘S to hide the sidebar when you want the whole width for the diff.

## The command palette

Press ⌘K to open the command palette. Everything the app can do is in it:
switching branches, fetching and pushing, opening the bottom pane's surfaces,
or starting a new session.

Two searches are also one keystroke away:

- Press ⇧⌘O, or Shift twice, to find a file by name.
- Press ⇧⌘F to search the text of every file.

In the palette, Backspace on an empty search goes back up a level, and Escape
closes it.
