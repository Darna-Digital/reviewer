## Choosing what to compare

The comparison chip sits under the branch chip in the sidebar. Press `t` to
open it and choose what your branch is compared against. Press `T` to switch
between your uncommitted changes and the last branch you compared against.

To remember the comparison for this branch, press ⌃R in the picker. Reviewer
then opens this branch on the same comparison next time, in both apps.

To read one commit, pick it in History. See [Branches and commits](/docs/terminal/git#history).

## Reading the diff

Press ⌥⌘2 to switch to **Review**, or ⌘G to switch between Browse and Review.
The sidebar lists the changed files, and the editor shows their diff.

| Key            | Does                                         |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| `j` `k`        | Next and previous line                       |
| `⌃D` `⌃U`      | Half a page down and up                      |
| `]` `[`        | Next and previous file                       |
| `}` `{`        | Next and previous hunk                       |
| `z`            | Fold the file                                |
| `s`            | Switch between split and unified             |
| `E`            | Switch between changed hunks and full files  |
| `W`            | Wrap long lines                              |

With wrap off, long lines scroll sideways. Use `←` and `→`, a sideways swipe,
or ⇧ and the scroll wheel.

In a split diff, `h` and `l` move between the old and new sides.

Press `f` in the sidebar to filter the changed files by name.

## Browsing the project

Press ⌥⌘1 for **Browse**. The sidebar shows the whole project, and files open
in tabs along the top of the editor. `]` and `[` move between tabs and ⌃W
closes one. Press `H` on a file in the sidebar to see its history.
